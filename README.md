# Northfield & Co. — E-Commerce Analytics Case Study

## Project Overview
This repository contains an end-to-end e-commerce performance analysis for **Northfield & Co.** (a US-based casual apparel retailer). The study covers daily sales, customer acquisition metrics, promotional events, and multi-channel marketing performance over an 852-day period (April 2024 – July 2026).

## Key Executive Highlights
* **Total Online Revenue:** Generated **$52.9M** in net revenue across the analyzed timeframe.
* **Customer Mix:** **67.5% ($35.7M)** of revenue was driven by returning loyalty members, while **32.5% ($17.2M)** came from first-time buyers.
* **Marketing Efficiency:** Spent **$4.21M** across paid advertising channels, achieving a overall blended **12.55x ROAS**.

---

## Strategic Findings & Recommendations

### 1. High-Impact Promotional Events
* **Black Friday / Cyber Monday (BFCM):** Generated the highest revenue spike at an average of **$170.9K/day** (over 3.5x higher than standard days).
* **Loyalty Mailings (UWG):** Direct email and mail campaigns consistently boosted daily revenue to **$116.4K/day** at minimal incremental ad spend.
* **Action:** Scale the frequency of loyalty mailings during non-promotional mid-quarter lulls to elevate baseline sales.

### 2. Marketing Channel Optimization
* **Primary Drivers:** Google Search/PMAX (56.2% of spend) and Meta Advantage+ (28.9% of spend) represent 85% of total paid acquisition and drive the highest net revenue volume.
* **Underperforming Channels:** Affiliate, Criteo, and Outbrain channels show diminishing returns relative to direct ad spend.
* **Action:** Reallocate low-performing display ad budgets into top-funnel Meta Prospecting to maintain new customer acquisition momentum.
